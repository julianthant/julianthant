## Hi, I'm Julian

I'm a CS student at UC San Diego. I've worked in HR offices, where I took the initiative to automate a lot of the workflows, including onboarding, separations and voicemail transcription. On the side, I'm building Tether and Driftwood.

## What I'm building

- **[HR-Relay](https://github.com/julianthant/hr-relay)**: In my current HR job, onboardings, separations and employee lookups meant clicking through a pile of university systems by hand. HR-Relay logs in and does the clicking for you. If it isn't sure a submission went through, it stops and asks a person.
- **[Tether](https://github.com/julianthant/tether)**: a way to let a coding agent work on its own for hours without giving it the whole machine. It can only touch files in one folder, and it saves its progress, so a killed run picks up where it left off.
- **[Driftwood](https://github.com/julianthant/driftwood)**: a small research pipeline for US stocks. The plan is to train simple models on daily data and only test them on periods they've never seen. Then a C++ simulator shows how much fees and slippage eat into the results. Nothing gets paper traded until I approve it.
